# Spec: operations-and-supervision

**Objective.** Let executives and operations leads find the next risk or decision, act on a specific object, and return to the correct queue.

**Pages.** P13–P25, P93–P94. The route inventory is adjustable after the disposition review.

**Requirements.** P13 leads with a short operational summary, actionable risk queue, and a route to deeper performance analysis; every metric names period/unit and destination. P20 becomes a priority-sorted queue, not equal blocks for AI draft, KPIs, shift, broadcast, and current-live counts; shift control stays beside the queue, broadcast is secondary, and summary metrics link to P13. P21 exposes “主播名单 / 主播动态” as named sibling views; default to the roster, render activity as a time-grouped inbox with object/state/action, preserve filters and position. P22 anchors the selected streamer and next action; overview/trend/plan-review/collaboration remain visible as explicit sections or destinations after comparing wayfinding. P23 separates edit → preview → confirm/send and retains drafts. P24 presents result → human judgment → next action, with evidence on demand. P25 groups received/initiated/all handoffs, with owner and receipt state. P93 prioritizes decisions unique to the director; avoid duplicating P13 metrics. P94 defaults to an actionable queue; member and team-data views are explicit siblings, not dropdown headings.

**Acceptance.** Representative boss/operator/lead/director flows can identify a risk, open the exact target, act, see the changed state, and return with list filters and position intact. Every page has a clear primary task and avoids duplicated summaries without an identified owner.
