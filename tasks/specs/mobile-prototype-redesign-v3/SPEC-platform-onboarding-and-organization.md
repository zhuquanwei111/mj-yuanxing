# Spec: platform-onboarding-and-organization

**Objective.** Make the platform, guild, onboarding, and organization routes understandable to the correct role, with forms and decisions presented in user task order.

**Pages.** P00–P12, P95–P96.

**Requirements.** P00 groups role entry by actual job task and describes each role plainly. P01 separates application list from one-application review, with material status and decision controls together. P02 clarifies selected guild, package/state and management destinations. P03 puts actionable platform issues before a concise business summary; ranking fields become vertical labeled records, not a hidden disclosure plus horizontal table. P10 divides required information into clear stages, places validation near fields, preserves values, and keeps submit available with the keyboard. P11 shows current/remaining step and next action. P12 organizes permissions by resource with view/edit meaning explicit. P95 makes every group’s owner, member count and risk visible without opening each group; details route to a group/member view. P96 is a standalone explanation/learning page, not a required stop in work flows.

**Acceptance.** Representative entry→application or organization→group/member→action flows are navigable and return to the correct context. Every required field and permission remains available after regrouping; empty/denied/locked states explain next steps. No core route is hidden behind the evaluation sidebar.

**Reference trade-offs.** Use image 5’s form/detail hierarchy and image 4’s stable rows, plus the previously captured BrightMirror reviewer shell only for prototype navigation/notes. Do not copy promotional onboarding art or game ranking visuals.
