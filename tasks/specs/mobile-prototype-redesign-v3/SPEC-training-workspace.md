# Spec: training-workspace

**Objective.** Make “who needs coaching or assessment now, what evidence is needed, and what happens after scoring” the organizing structure of training.

**Pages.** P40, P41, P41D, P42.

**Requirements.** P40 shows today’s coaching and overdue assessments before aggregate counts; completed history and course administration have distinct destinations. P41 prioritizes active learner tasks and current progress; course editing/history do not occupy the same primary zone. P41D anchors one learner, current stage, evidence, risks, and next task. P42 defaults to pending scores/retakes; a scoring view focuses on one learner and one course, explains the criteria where needed, and returns a clear result plus next step. Progress appears only if it changes the user’s decision.

**Acceptance.** Trainer can go from today’s task to a learner, review relevant evidence, score or assign remediation, see the new state, then return without losing selected learner or queue position. Every listed course/assessment remains reachable after layout changes; no required task relies on a collapsed title.

**Reference trade-offs.** Use course progress cues from image 3 and detail action hierarchy from image 5; do not copy promotional hero banners or horizontal course carousels.
